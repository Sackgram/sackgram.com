Support

Questions, problems, privacy requests, or anything else about Sackgram.

Email: info@sackgram.com

We aim to reply within 5 business days. Please write in English or Korean.

What to include

It helps us if you tell us:

  • Your device and operating system version (for example, "Galaxy S23, Android 16").
  • The app version, shown in the app's Settings screen.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Please do not send us your password, your security PIN, or the contents of your conversations. We do not need them, and we cannot read your messages in any case.

Account and privacy requests

To ask what information is associated with your account, to request deletion, or to exercise any other right described in our Privacy Policy, email us at the address above from any address you can be reached at, and describe your request.

Because Sackgram accounts are not tied to a phone number or email address, we may not be able to verify that an account belongs to you. Where we cannot verify ownership, we may be unable to act on a request — this is a consequence of collecting so little about you, and we would rather refuse an unverifiable request than act on one made by the wrong person.

Deleting your data yourself

You can do most of this in the app without contacting us:

  • Delete a message you sent, for both sides.
  • Set a conversation to delete messages automatically.
  • Remove a contact, which also deletes that conversation's history for both people.
  • Leave a group.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes the keys held on your device.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, email info@sackgram.com with the details and, if possible, steps to reproduce it. Please give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate before disclosing it publicly.
