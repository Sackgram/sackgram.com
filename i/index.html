What Sackgram is

Sackgram is a private messenger for Android and iOS, built by Sackgram Labs, Inc. Message content is encrypted on your device and decrypted on the other person's, and creating an account does not ask for your phone number or your email address.

There is more on the home page, including what we do not claim.

Getting the app

Sackgram is not publicly released yet, so there is nothing to install from an app store today. When there is, the links will be on this page and on the home page.

If you would like to be told when it is available, email info@sackgram.com.

After you install it

This page does not read your code. The part of the link that identifies it stays in your browser and is never sent to us.
