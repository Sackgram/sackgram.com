Messaging and calling, without handing over your phone number.

Sackgram is a private messenger for Android and iOS, built by Sackgram Labs, Inc.

What Sackgram does

  • End-to-end encrypted messages. Message content is encrypted on your device and decrypted on the recipient's. We cannot read it.
  • Sign up without a phone number. Creating an account does not require a phone number or an email address. You choose a SACKGRAM ID instead.
  • Messages that disappear. You can set a conversation to delete its messages automatically after a chosen period, or delete a message you sent for both sides.
  • Voice calls. One-to-one calls between people who have connected.
  • You add people deliberately. There is no directory and no search. You connect by sharing a link or QR code, or through a mutual contact — nobody can find you by guessing.
  • Group chats. Encrypted group conversations with people you have already connected with.
  • Screenshot blocking on Android. Chats and media viewers ask Android to block screenshots and screen recording on the device showing them.

What we do not claim. Sackgram protects the content of your messages, and we have designed it to ask for as little about you as we can. It cannot protect you from someone photographing their screen with another camera, and it does not make you anonymous on the internet. We would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise.

Availability

Sackgram is in development and is not yet publicly released. This site will be updated when the app is available.
